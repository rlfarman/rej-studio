## Search for a gene

Type into the search box on the homepage. REJ Studio accepts gene
symbols, full names, Ensembl IDs, MIM numbers, and disease terms.
Results are ranked — exact symbol hits first, then name prefixes, then
full-text matches, then substring fallbacks. Most searches return in
under a second.

See [Search syntax](/docs/reference/search-syntax) for the full list
of accepted query formats.

## Filter by species

A species filter lives in the sidebar. The default is "all species" —
toggling to human-only or mouse-only scopes every search, the
recent-genes list, and favorites. The filter persists across sessions.

## Browse isoforms

Clicking a search result opens the gene detail page. You'll see:

- **Header** — symbol, full name, chromosome location, and external
  links (Ensembl, UniProt, MIM).
- **Isoform list** — every annotated transcript, sorted by length.
  Each row shows the transcript ID, biotype, length, and exon count.
- **Isoform actions** — download the CDS or protein sequence as FASTA,
  or open the isoform directly in the design tool.

## Use favorites and recents

- Click the star on a gene to favorite it. Favorites appear in the
  sidebar for quick access.
- Every gene you open is added to the **recent** list, capped at 20.

Both are stored in your browser — clearing site data wipes them.

## Open an isoform in the design tool

Click **Open in design tool** on any isoform row. The CDS, gene
symbol, and species are pre-filled. From there, see
[Design a sequence](/docs/guides/design-sequence).
