---
title: Searching for a gene
description: How the search works and what you can type into it.
---

The search box on the homepage accepts any of the following:

- **Gene symbol** — `ATM`, `TP53`, `BRCA1`
- **Gene name** — `ataxia telangiectasia mutated`
- **Ensembl ID** — `ENSG00000149311` (gene) or `ENST00000278616` (transcript)
- **MIM number** — `607585`
- **Disease / phenotype term** — `ataxia`, `breast cancer`

Matches are ranked: exact symbol hits come first, then name prefixes, then
full-text matches on descriptions and disease terms, then substring
fallbacks. Most searches return in under a second.

## Filtering by species

A species filter lives in the sidebar. The default is "all species" —
toggling to human-only or mouse-only scopes every search, the recent-genes
list, and favorites. The filter persists across sessions.

## From search to isoforms

Clicking a result takes you to the gene's detail page. You'll see:

- **Header** — symbol, full name, chromosome location, and external links
  (Ensembl, UniProt, MIM).
- **Isoform list** — every annotated transcript, sorted by length. Each row
  shows the transcript ID, biotype, length, and exon count.
- **Isoform actions** — download the CDS or protein sequence as FASTA, or
  open the isoform directly in the design tool.

## Favorites and recent genes

- Click the star on a gene to favorite it. Favorites appear in the sidebar
  for quick access.
- Every gene you open is added to the **recent** list, capped at 20.

Both are stored in your browser — clearing site data wipes them.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` — open the command palette from anywhere
- `/` — focus the search box on the homepage
- `Esc` — close the command palette
