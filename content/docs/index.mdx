REJ Studio helps researchers design **RNA End-Joining (REJ)** sequences from
coding DNA. Search for a gene, inspect its isoforms, pick a transcript, and run
it through a configurable optimization pipeline — all in the browser.

## What you can do

- **Search** human and mouse genes by symbol, name, Ensembl ID, or disease
  association.
- **Browse** isoforms for a gene, compare lengths, and download coding or
  protein sequences.
- **Design** an optimized sequence by choosing a transcript (or pasting your
  own CDS) and tuning the optimization constraints.
- **Download** the optimized sequence and an optimization report when the job
  finishes.

## A note on scope

REJ Studio is a research tool. The optimization pipeline is deterministic
given the same inputs and constraints, but the biology downstream of the
sequence is not — validate any designed sequence experimentally before use.
