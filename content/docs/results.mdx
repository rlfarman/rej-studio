---
title: Reading your results
description: What you get back after an optimization job finishes and how to interpret it.
---

When a job finishes you'll see three things: the **optimized sequence**,
an **optimization report**, and a set of **diagnostic badges**.

## The optimized sequence

A coding-equivalent rewrite of your input — same protein, different DNA.
Download it as FASTA using the button in the header. The filename includes
the gene symbol (or `Custom` if you pasted your own) so you can stack
multiple designs without collisions.

## The optimization report

The report is a per-constraint summary of what the solver did. For each
constraint you enabled you'll see:

- Whether it was **satisfied**, **partially satisfied**, or **violated**.
- The **score** the solver ended on (lower is better for most constraints).
- A short **explanation** of what changed — e.g. "3 CpG sites removed",
  "splice site at position 412 neutralized", "GC window tightened to
  54% across bases 200–250".

A constraint that shows up as "violated" is usually either (a) impossible
given your other constraints, or (b) impossible given the amino acid
sequence. The report will tell you which. Relaxing or disabling the
offending constraint and re-running is the normal fix.

## Diagnostic badges

The header shows a few quick-read indicators:

- **GC%** — overall GC content. Green if inside your target range.
- **CpG count** — number of CpG dinucleotides. Lower is usually better.
- **Max k-mer repeat** — the most-repeated k-mer. Helpful for spotting
  complexity issues at a glance.
- **Length** — total bp, for sanity-checking against your input.

These badges are a summary, not a substitute for the full report. If you
care about a specific constraint, read its report entry.

## What to do next

- **Save the sequence.** Results live in the browser session. Download the
  FASTA before closing the tab.
- **Re-run with tweaks.** The form remembers your last parameters. Adjust
  one thing at a time and compare reports to understand which constraint
  was doing what.
- **Validate experimentally.** The optimizer is deterministic given the
  same input, but biology isn't. Nothing in the report predicts
  expression, stability, or function — that still has to be measured.
