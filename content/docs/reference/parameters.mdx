## Input

### Species

Sets the codon usage table the optimizer adapts to. Pick the organism
you'll express the sequence in — human and mouse have different usage
preferences and the wrong choice will push codon optimization in the
wrong direction.

### Splice junction

The position where REJ Studio splits your CDS into 5' and 3'
fragments. The slider defaults to the
[WGGW motif](/docs/reference/glossary#wggw-motif) closest to the
midpoint — a balanced split with a valid RNA End-Joining substrate at
the joint. Drag it to pick a different motif if you have a reason to.

## Soft objectives

Soft objectives are things the solver _tries_ to optimize. Each has a
weight controlling how much it pulls on the final sequence relative to
the others. See [weights](#parameter-weights) below.

### Remove cryptic splice sites [#remove-cryptic-splice-sites]

Scans each fragment for cryptic 5' and 3' splice site motifs and
rewrites them to break the motif without changing the protein.
Recommended for any design intended to end up in a transcribed
context — unintended splicing can silently delete internal exons.

### Minimize CpG sites [#minimize-cpgs]

Reduces the number of
[CpG dinucleotides](/docs/reference/glossary#cpg-dinucleotide). CpGs
are substrates for methylation and can trigger innate immune sensing.
Lowering them is standard practice for therapeutic RNA and synthetic
constructs.

### Reduce k-mer complexity [#kmer-complexity]

Penalizes repeated 10-mers across the sequence.
[High-complexity sequences](/docs/reference/glossary#k-mer-complexity)
synthesize more reliably and behave better during manufacturing and
sequencing.

## Hard constraints

Hard constraints must be satisfied. If the solver can't meet one, the
job reports it as violated.

### Enforce 35-60% GC content [#gc-content]

Keeps GC content inside the mammalian-friendly range over a sliding
window. The GC sparkline visualizes how content varies across the
sequence — the green band marks the 40-60% target:

If the solver can't hit the window given your other constraints,
you'll see it called out in the report. Leave this on unless you
have a specific reason to run without a GC constraint.

## Stimulatory introns

Optional. REJ Studio can insert a short optimized intron into either
fragment to boost expression. The protein is unchanged but each intron
adds length.

### 5' stimulatory intron [#stim-5]

Inserts an intron into the 5' fragment. Useful when the 5' half is
the limiting piece for expression.

### 3' stimulatory intron [#stim-3]

Inserts an intron into the 3' fragment. Useful when the 3' half is
the limiting piece.

Enable one, both, or neither based on downstream context. They stack
cleanly with the rest of the optimization.

## Parameter weights [#parameter-weights]

Each soft objective has a weight that controls how much it pulls on
the final sequence relative to the others. You only need to touch
these when the defaults give you a result that's good at one thing
and weak at another.

| Weight | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Codon optimization** | How hard the solver pushes toward the species-preferred codon table. |
| **Cryptic splice site** | How hard it pushes to eliminate splice-like motifs. |
| **CpG minimization** | How hard it pushes CpG count down. |
| **K-mer complexity** | How hard it pushes to break up repeated k-mers. |

Hard constraints (GC content) always take priority over any weight.
