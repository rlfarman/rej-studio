## Design Context

### Users

Academic researchers, biotech industry scientists, and lab technicians optimizing genes using RNA-fragment end-joining technology. They work at benches and in offices, doing both quick gene lookups and deep analysis/design sessions. They're accustomed to clunky bioinformatics tools and will be pleasantly surprised by a polished, modern experience.

### Brand Personality

**Precise, modern, approachable.** The interface should make scientists think "Wow, this is a really cool and clean tool — I'm not used to using things this nice." It should feel like a premium modern product (think ChatGPT/Claude-level polish) rather than a typical academic tool.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Visual tone**: Clean and modern with a touch of flair. Taken seriously but not sterile.
- **References**: ChatGPT, Claude — conversational AI products with polished, approachable interfaces
- **Anti-references**: Old-school bioinformatics tools (BLAST, UCSC Genome Browser). Nothing plain, boring, or dated.
- **Theme**: Both light and dark mode are equally important
- **Delight level**: Middle ground — professional enough to be taken seriously, but with subtle moments of flair and personality that make it memorable

### Design Principles

1. **Elevate the science**: Make complex genomic data feel approachable without dumbing it down
2. **Surprise with polish**: Every interaction should feel intentional and crafted — scientists deserve nice tools too
3. **Subtle flair over flash**: Delight through refined micro-interactions and thoughtful details, not gimmicks
4. **Respect the workflow**: Never let aesthetics slow down the task at hand
5. **Modern but trustworthy**: Feel cutting-edge without sacrificing the credibility a research tool needs
